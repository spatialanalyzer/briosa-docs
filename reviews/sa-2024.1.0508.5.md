## Method and Results

The comparison matched all 1,283 group-qualified 2024 commands to separate
canonical entries. Repeated names were disambiguated by their observed groups;
no renamed or unrelated command identities were merged. All 1,282 distinct step
labels also occur in the later capture; the two Save commands remain distinct.

The comparison covered input/output direction, argument labels, call arity,
SDK helper names, enumerated choices, and sample values. Only whitespace and
known exporter representation differences were normalized for comparison;
the unnormalized differences were reviewed separately.

Both canonical interop manifests are identical except for their target-specific
managed assembly version line. All 151 methods, parameter metadata, dispatch
identifiers, interface identities, and class metadata agree. This is static
interface evidence, not runtime or cross-target compatibility validation.

There are 39 individually reviewed difference entries: 23 argument/call-shape
differences, 14 choice-list differences (one overlaps the shape group), and
three sample-only differences. The three extra vector-group arguments are
exporter anomalies rather than new MP inputs. The shared collection-object
setter variation was reviewed as one family.

Final dispositions: 996 Next, 171 Excluded, 75 SDK Unavailable, 41 Uncommitted,
zero Current, and zero Undecided. Three newly unavailable scanning bindings
account for the only changes to inherited support selection. SDK Unavailable
describes the lack of a complete reviewed mapping and can be revisited when
new exact-target evidence is available.
